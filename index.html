Q3: How to identify genuine IS:710 Marine Plywood in India?

Genuine IS:710 Marine Plywood must display the official BIS ISI mark with a valid 7-digit CM/L license number. It uses unextended Phenol Formaldehyde resin (visible by dark phenolic glue lines), 100% imported Gurjan or calibrated hardwood cores with zero core gaps, and survives 72 continuous hours in boiling water without delamination.
